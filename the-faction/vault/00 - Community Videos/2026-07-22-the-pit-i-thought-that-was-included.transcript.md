# Transcript — "I thought that was included."

> "I thought that was included."

Your AI quoted that client but it did not scope the project. And now your client just said, I thought that was included. And you have no document that says it wasn't. Well that sentence has ended more client relationships and killed more builder businesses than any bug, any outage, and any security breach ever will. Because now it's your word against theirs and you have nothing in writing.

So here are the three things you're going to direct your AI to build into every proposal before you send another one out. Step one, an out of scope section that is longer than the scope section. Most builders list what they will build and never define what they won't. That is where every client dispute starts and ends. Your AI can draft the deliverables in three minutes.

It will never draft the exclusions unless you tell it what they are. The exclusions are what protect you and the client's expectations grow past what you agreed to build. Scope creep is exactly where this all happens. So in step two, a change order process with a trigger and a price. When the client wants something that is not in the original scope, there has to be a documented path for how that gets added, what it's gonna cost, and what it does to the timeline.

Without it, you're either saying no and damaging the relationship or saying yes and working for free. That's a no no. Your AI can build a change order template that makes scope creep feel professional instead of confrontational. And step three, acceptance criteria for every deliverable. Not build me a dashboard.

What does the dashboard display? What does it connect to? What does the client sign off on to confirm that it's done? Without acceptance criteria, the project might never finish because the client can always say, that's not what I meant. And your AI will build exactly what you tell it.

If you did not define done, neither did your AI. Your AI will write a beautiful proposal. It will never protect you from the conversation that happens after the client signs it. That's your job. So direct your AI to help you do it the right way, and that's a win.
