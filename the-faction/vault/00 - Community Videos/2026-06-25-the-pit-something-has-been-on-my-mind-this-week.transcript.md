# Transcript — Something has been on my mind this week.

> Something has been on my mind this week.

Something has been on my mind this week. There are a lot of certifications out there right now. Every platform has one. Every bootcamp offers one. Some of them are solid.

Most of them test whether you can memorize documentation. The problem is not the certifications. The problem is what they're certifying. Knowing how to configure a tool is not the same as knowing why you would choose that particular tool. Knowing how to deploy a platform is not the same as knowing what happens when that platform goes down.

Knowing how to write a prompt is not the same as knowing what to do when the model hallucinates in production. The industry is building a generation of builders who can operate the tools but cannot design the systems in total. And that gap is going to show up in the next eighteen months when these applications hit real users with real expectations and real data. That is why what we do here is a little bit different. We do not certify tool proficiency.

We certify architectural understanding. Thirteen layers, three tiers, all evidence based. Because the market does not need more people who can click buttons and understand Claude completely. It needs people who know what happens after that button gets clicked on any platform with any tool anywhere. And that's AI directed engineering.
