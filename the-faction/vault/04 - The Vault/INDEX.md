# The Vault

## Why We Built The Vault

Every builder in The Faction is shipping faster than traditional developers ever could. You're directing AI to stand up apps, APIs, and databases in hours, not weeks.

That speed is your superpower. It's also your biggest risk.

The apps you're building handle real user data, real payment flows, real authentication. If your AI builds an insecure endpoint or your product crosses a regulatory line, you own that. Not the AI. You.

The Vault exists because security and compliance can't be an afterthought when you're shipping at this pace. These courses give you the foundations to direct your AI tools with confidence. You'll understand what to ask for, what to verify, what to never skip, and which rules actually apply to what you're building.

Two courses. Fourteen modules. Fourteen exams. Two specialist certifications that prove you build fast, build secure, and build legal.

## Three Courses at The Vault

**Security Foundations for Builders** targets the gap between "I can ship" and "I can ship without getting hacked." Seven modules covering your app's attack surface, API security, database protection, authentication, secrets management, transport security, and vulnerability scanning. You'll know what to verify before anything goes live.

**Compliance Foundations for Builders** targets the gap between "I built it" and "I built it legally." Seven modules covering the regulatory landscape, which laws apply to your build, data residency and cross-border rules, industry-specific trigger points, compliance documentation systems, when to bring in professionals, and shipping a compliance map for your product.

**Vibecoder Security Audit Method** targets the gap between "I can spot a vulnerability" and "I can audit a codebase for money." Seven modules covering why AI-generated code fails differently, the 13-layer audit framework, automated scanning direction, manual review hotspots, findings and severity and reporting, remediation sprints, and running a complete codebase audit end to end. This is the method behind The Faction Group's Rapid Platform Audit.

## What These Courses Cover

### Security Foundations

**Your App's Attack Surface.** What attackers actually target in AI-directed builds, and the specific vulnerabilities that come from shipping fast with generated code.

**API Security Basics.** Authentication, authorization, rate limiting, and input validation — the four walls that keep your endpoints from becoming open doors.

**Protecting Your Database.** SQL injection, access controls, encryption at rest, and the backup discipline that means one bad query doesn't end your business.

**Authentication Done Right.** Sessions, tokens, OAuth, MFA — the identity layer that determines whether your users are who they say they are.

**Secrets Management.** API keys, database credentials, environment variables — where they live, how they leak, and the practices that keep them out of your git history.

**Transport Security.** TLS, certificate management, and the encryption layer between your users and your servers that makes the difference between private and public.

**Vulnerability Scanning.** Automated tools, dependency audits, and the scanning discipline that catches what you missed before someone else does.

### Compliance Foundations

**The Regulatory Landscape Map.** The major frameworks that govern software products — GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, SOC 2 — and why builders need to understand them even if they'll never be lawyers.

**Which Laws Apply To Your Build.** A decision framework for figuring out which regulations actually apply to your specific product, your users, and your data flows.

**Data Residency and Cross-Border Rules.** Where your data lives, where your users are, and why those two facts determine which rules you follow.

**Industry-Specific Trigger Points.** Healthcare, finance, education, children's data — the verticals where one wrong move triggers regulations most builders have never heard of.

**Compliance Documentation Systems.** The records, policies, and audit trails that prove you're compliant — not just to regulators, but to the enterprise customers who will ask before they buy.

**When To Bring In Professionals.** The line between "I can handle this" and "I need a lawyer" — and the cost of getting that judgment call wrong.

**Ship: Compliance Map For Your Product.** A complete compliance map for your actual product, built from everything in the course. The deliverable that proves you know what rules apply and how you're following them.

### Vibecoder Security Audit Method

**Why AI-Generated Code Fails Differently.** Pattern replication, training data leakage, hallucinated security, missing defense-in-depth, cross-session drift, and the false confidence effect. The threat model the whole method answers.

**The 13-Layer Audit Framework.** The complete CADE stack from Frontend through Availability, the core questions at each layer, and RED / YELLOW / GREEN grading calibrated to the business you are auditing.

**Automated Scanning Direction.** Dependency scanners, static analysis, secret scanning across git history, deployed header and TLS checks, and cloud config auditing. Parsed, deduplicated, and filtered into signal.

**Manual Review Hotspots.** Business logic flaws, auth boundary testing, endpoint enumeration, client-side trust, and configuration review. The findings no scanner will ever hand you.

**Findings, Severity and Reporting.** The five severity levels, the four-part finding a founder can act on, and a report structure that leads with posture and the plan instead of a body count.

**Remediation Sprints.** Prioritizing by severity and dependency, fixing with the finding as context, verifying by re-scanning, and guarding against regression in code that will be regenerated.

**Ship: Complete Codebase Audit.** Scoping, running the full workflow in order, communicating during the engagement, delivering without panic, and positioning the audit as a recurring relationship.

## How It Works

Each module has a study guide and an exam. The study guide covers what you need to know and gives you the AI audit prompts to check your own work. The exam is 25 scenario-based questions at 80% to pass. No memorization. You either understand the judgment call or you don't.

Pass all seven exams in a course and you earn the corresponding Vault Specialist badge. Those badges count toward your CADE Specialist and CADE Distinguished credentials.

Start with Module 1 of either course and work through in order. Each module builds on the one before it.
