# The Launchpad

## Why We Built This

The certification proved you can build. The Launchpad proves you can run the business around it — and that you can validate the right idea before you invest months building the wrong one.

Most builders who finish their CADE certification hit the same wall: they can direct AI to ship real software, but nobody taught them how to find the client, price the work, deliver it without chaos, or prove it publicly. And nobody taught them how to figure out whether their idea is worth building at all before they start. The technical gap is closed. The business gap and the validation gap are where careers stall.

The Launchpad exists because we watched it happen. Builders with real skills sitting idle because they pitched technology instead of outcomes. Builders undercharging because they priced off hours instead of value. Builders pouring months into products nobody wanted because they skipped validation. The Launchpad closes both gaps with two courses that give you the business infrastructure and the validation discipline to launch with confidence.

## Two Courses at The Launchpad

**Business Operations for Builders** targets the gap between "I can build" and "I run a builder business." Seven modules covering client acquisition, pricing, delivery, portfolio, scaling, legal foundations, and the growth flywheel that compounds so you stop starting from zero every month.

**Validate an Idea Before You Build It** targets the gap between "I have an idea" and "I have evidence this idea is worth building." Seven modules covering the validation mindset, problem validation, solution validation, market sizing, competitive analysis, the validation decision framework, and shipping your validated idea brief.

## What These Courses Cover

### Business Operations for Builders

**Finding Your First Client.** Where paying clients actually come from, how to pitch with no track record, and how to turn one project into the next three.

**Pricing Your Builds.** Modular pricing, value anchoring, written scope, and the discipline to trade instead of discount.

**The Client Delivery Playbook.** SOW to kickoff to milestones to handoff, run as a repeatable system that keeps promises and protects relationships.

**Building Your Portfolio.** Proof that converts strangers into clients. Case studies, demo videos, and a portfolio that generates inquiries instead of compliments.

**Scaling Past Solo.** Subcontractors, small teams, and quality that doesn't depend on you being the one who builds everything.

**Legal and Financial Basics.** Entities, contracts, liability, invoicing, and taxes. The boring systems that prevent disasters.

**The Builder's Growth Flywheel.** Content, referrals, community, and the reputation loop that compounds so you stop starting from zero every month.

### Validate an Idea Before You Build It

**The Validation Mindset.** Why most builders skip validation, the cost of building the wrong thing, and the mental shift from "I think this will work" to "I have evidence this will work."

**Problem Validation.** Finding real problems worth solving, talking to potential users without leading them, and distinguishing genuine pain from polite interest.

**Solution Validation.** Testing whether your proposed solution actually solves the validated problem, before you write a line of code.

**Market Sizing and Demand Signals.** Estimating whether the opportunity is big enough to justify the build, and reading the signals that say "go" or "stop."

**Competitive Landscape Analysis.** Understanding who else is solving this problem, where the gaps are, and whether your angle gives you a real advantage.

**The Validation Decision Framework.** Pulling all the evidence together into a structured go/no-go decision you can defend to yourself and to stakeholders.

**Ship: Your Validated Idea Brief.** Packaging your validation work into a brief that becomes the foundation for everything you build next.

## Who This Is For

Builders who are ready to get paid and ready to build the right thing. Whether you just finished the CADE certification or you've been building and need the business infrastructure and validation discipline underneath, these courses fill the gap between "I can build" and "I run a builder business that builds what people actually want."

These are free courses. Every member of The Faction gets access. No gate, no upsell. The business foundations and validation skills are too important to put behind a paywall.

## How It Works

Each module has a study guide and an exam. The study guide covers what you need to know and gives you the AI audit prompts to check your own work. The exam is 25 scenario-based questions at 80% to pass. No memorization. You either understand the judgment call or you don't.

Pass all seven exams in a course and you earn the corresponding Launchpad Specialist badge. Those badges count toward your CADE Specialist and CADE Distinguished credentials.

Start with Module 1 of either course and work through in order. Each module builds on the one before it.
