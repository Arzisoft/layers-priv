# START HERE

Watch the welcome video above, then follow these steps to get started.

## 1. INTRODUCE YOURSELF

Head to the Welcome to The Faction forum and tell the community who you are and what you're building. Builders who show up get shown up for.

## 2. START YOUR FIRST LAYER

Go to The Foundation. Open the Layer 01 study guide — it reads right on the page, nothing to download. Take the exam when you're ready. Pass it and earn the badge — but don't wait for permission. Every layer is already open.

## 3. EXPLORE YOUR SPACES

Drop into "The Pit" to keep up with your peers day-to-day.

Explore "The Forge" for all of Matt's proven frameworks, reference materials, and the tools behind every layer of the stack.

Study "The Industry" before you build anything. Education, construction, and healthcare — how each vertical actually runs, and the compliance lines you can't cross. Knowing them up front beats rebuilding around them later.

Open "The Launchpad" when you're ready to get paid for it. Finding your first client, pricing your builds, and the legal and financial basics of turning what you build into a business.

Every one of them is already in your account.

## 4. GO AS FAR AS YOU WANT

The Mastery, The Industry, The Vault, The Launchpad and The Frontier are open to you right now. No tiers to unlock, nothing to upgrade to. Seventy-seven courses, and all of it is already in your account.

Work as deep as you want. Every exam you pass earns a badge, so you can see exactly what you have absorbed and what is still ahead of you.

Go where the work takes you — the stack you are building on, the vertical you are selling into, or the part you keep getting stuck on.

## YOUR NEXT STEPS

• Introduce yourself in the Welcome to The Faction forum

• Go to The Foundation and open your Layer 01 study guide

• Take your first exam

• Explore the spaces

• Go anywhere. Foundation to Frontier, day one.

Now get out there and build something.
