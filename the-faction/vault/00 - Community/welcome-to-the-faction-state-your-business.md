Drop a comment below — keep it real:

1. Who are you? Name and where you're operating from.

2. What are you building? Business, project, department — whatever you're working on right now.

3. What's your setup? Solo operator? Small crew? Running teams?

5. What brought you here? The certification? The frameworks? A crew that actually builds? Say it plain.
