I was scrolling our app intake pool yesterday and I saw a product launch that caught my eye.

Beautiful product.
Great demo video.
Polished landing page.
And The comments were glowing.

Upon initial review of the product within thirty seconds
I found three things that would keep me up at night if I were that founder.

No rate limiting on their API.

Their auth tokens had no expiration.

And their error messages were returning stack traces in prod.

This is not a criticism. This is the gap we see so often in vibe coded apps.

The gap between a product that looks ready and a product that is ready.

And this is exactly why the anatomy matters more than the ambition.

That founder is talented.

The product solves a real problem.

The market clearly wants it.

But the first time someone with bad intentions finds that public API,

the conversation changes from growth metrics to incident response.

In this community, we talk about the layers that nobody sees in the demo video.

Because the demo video gets you the launch.

The layers get you the second year.

That is the difference between a project and a business.
