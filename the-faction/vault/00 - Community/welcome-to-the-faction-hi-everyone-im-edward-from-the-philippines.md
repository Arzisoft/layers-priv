# Hi everyone, I’m Edward from the Philippines.

Hi everyone, I’m Edward from the Philippines.

I’m a Senior Web Designer, Developer, and AI Orchestrator with over 15 years of experience building digital experiences using WordPress, Shopify, UX/UI design, SEO, and web technologies. Recently, I’ve been shifting my focus toward AI-powered business systems, workflow automation, and product strategy.

I’m currently building **Launch Local PH**, an AI-powered platform designed to help Philippine MSMEs create a stronger digital presence through intelligent automation and practical business tools. My goal is to use AI not just to automate tasks, but to help people build better businesses and solve meaningful problems.

I joined The Faction because I want to learn from builders who are pushing AI beyond prompts and into real-world execution. I’m looking forward to exchanging ideas, sharing what I learn, and collaborating with others who believe AI can amplify human potential rather than replace it.

Looking forward to connecting with everyone.
