# A few years ago I had a conversation with a client that changed the way I scope

A few years ago I had a conversation with a client that changed the way I scope every project.

They came to me with a beautiful application.

Well-designed. Working features. Users were signing up.

The conversation was about scaling it.

I asked one question.

What is your biggest cost right now.

They said engineering. Which I expected.

Then I asked a second question.

What is your second biggest cost.

They could not answer.

They did not know their hosting costs.

They did not know their third-party API costs.

They did not know their egress costs.

The application was making money.

But they had no idea if the unit economics worked at ten times the current volume.

That conversation taught me something.

Builders think about features.

Business owners think about margins.

The best builders think about both.

From that point forward, every project I scope includes a cost model.

Not just what it costs to build.

But also What it costs to run.
At current scale. At ten x.
At a hundred x.

Because the application that scales and loses money at every level is not a business.

It is a very expensive hobby.

Think about the margins.
