# AI shopping agents are making purchase decisions on behalf of humans right now.

AI shopping agents are making purchase decisions on behalf of humans right now. Retail traffic from AI sources is up 393% year over year. Eight out of ten shoppers under 44 are using LLMs as their primary shopping tool and making decisions before they ever visit your website. If your product is invisible to AI agents, you are invisible to the fastest-growing customer channel on the planet. Today I walk through what to build so they can find you.

**PROMPT:** Direct your AI: "Audit my product's visibility to AI shopping agents with three steps: (1) Structured data audit. Review every product page, pricing page, and feature page on my website for machine-readable schema markup (JSON-LD, Open Graph, product schema). Identify every page missing structured data and generate the correct schema for each. (2) AI agent specification page. Build a machine-readable product specification document that covers: pricing tiers, feature list, integration capabilities, uptime guarantees, security certifications, data export options, supported currencies, and compliance standards. Format it as both a public webpage and a JSON endpoint an AI agent could query. (3) LLM discoverability test. Query ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini with 5 purchase-intent prompts in my product category (example: 'recommend a [product type] under $50/month for a small business'). Document whether my product appears in the response, how accurately it is described, and what information is missing or incorrect. Output a gap report with fixes."
