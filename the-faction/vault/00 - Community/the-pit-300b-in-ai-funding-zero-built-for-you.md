# $300B in AI Funding. Zero Built for You.

**$300B in AI Funding. Zero Built for You.**

Q1 2026 shattered every venture funding record in history. $300 billion in a single quarter. AI captured more than 80% of it.

Anthropic reached a $965 billion valuation. OpenAI hit $852 billion. Four companies absorbed 63% of all global venture capital in Q1. The AI infrastructure layer is being funded like it's the new internet. Because it is.

And precisely zero of those billions were built for you.

Not for the SMB owner figuring out which AI tool to use Monday morning. Not for the operator wondering whether to automate intake or reporting first. Not for the 12-person agency or the law firm with three partners trying to decide if AI is worth the disruption.

The capital is concentrated at the infrastructure and foundation model layer. The deployment layer, the people, processes, and readiness that help real businesses use the tools, is an afterthought.

Microsoft went GA with Agent 365. KPMG is deploying agents globally. NTT DATA and Google Cloud are rolling out 500 agents across enterprise industries. 100% of enterprises surveyed have agentic AI on their 2026 roadmap.

Every single one targets the Fortune 500.

Meanwhile, 70% of SMBs are stuck in the experimental phase. The technology inside those $965 billion companies is powerful and inaccessible to the businesses that make up the backbone of the economy.

This isn't a conspiracy. It's economics. Building an AI deployment practice for the HVAC company in Dallas doesn't attract a $330 million Series B. The unit economics don't scale the way VCs need them to.

But there are 33 million small businesses in the US. If even 10% need structured AI deployment support in the next two years, that's a market in the hundreds of billions. It just doesn't look like a single platform play.

The $300 billion isn't coming to help you. That was never the plan.

The money went to the machine. The opportunity is in what you build with it.
