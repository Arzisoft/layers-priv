# THE DM PLAYBOOK — A Complete System for Selling in the Chat

THE DM PLAYBOOK — A Complete System for Selling in the Chat

The old way: cold calls, cold emails, cold outreach. The new way: warm conversations that start in the DMs and end with a signed deal.

The DM Playbook is a 4-phase system for turning chat conversations into revenue — without being pushy, weird, or salesy.

PHASE 1: OPEN

The conversation starts with value, not a pitch. Someone engages with your content. They comment. They DM a keyword. They ask a question. Your job in Phase 1 is simple: acknowledge them, ask one question, and start a real conversation.

What works: "Hey, saw you commented on the automation post. What's the biggest bottleneck in your business right now?"

What doesn't: "Hey! Thanks for following! Here's my link!" That's not a conversation. That's spam.

PHASE 2: QUALIFY

Not everyone who DMs you is a buyer. And that's fine. Phase 2 is about asking the right questions to figure out if there's a real fit. Three questions max:

"What are you working on right now?"

"What's the biggest thing slowing you down?"

"Have you tried solving this before?"

If their answers align with what you offer, move to Phase 3. If they don't, give them value anyway and move on. Not every conversation is a sale. But every conversation is a relationship.

PHASE 3: DELIVER

This is where you give them a quick win. A framework. A resource. A specific piece of advice that applies to their situation. You're not selling yet — you're proving you can help.

The magic: when you help someone for free in the DMs, you create reciprocity. They didn't ask for a pitch. They got genuine value. Now they're thinking: "If the free stuff is this good, what does the paid stuff look like?"

PHASE 4: CLOSE

If Phase 3 landed, the close is natural. "I actually have a program that covers exactly this. Want me to send you the details?" That's it. No pressure. No urgency. No fake scarcity.

If they say yes, send the link. If they say not now, respect it. They'll come back when they're ready — because you didn't burn the relationship by being pushy.

THE RULES:

• Never pitch in the first message

• Always lead with a question

• Give real value before asking for anything

• One conversation at a time — don't batch DMs like emails

• If they're not a fit, say so honestly

The DM isn't a sales channel. It's a relationship channel that happens to convert when you do it right.
