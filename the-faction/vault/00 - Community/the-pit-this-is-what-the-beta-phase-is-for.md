# This is what the beta phase is for.

**This is what the beta phase is for.**

You don't launch a beta to prove your system is perfect. You launch a beta because no amount of internal testing replaces real users, in The Faction's case that's fast moving ambitious builders, on real devices, working through real problems. That's the whole point.

Earlier today, [Shaf Cangil](https://the-faction.mn.co/members/40648972) posted feedback about the study guide experience on mobile. She was direct about it, real direct, the way I love it. The content is hard to navigate on a phone screen. And she's right.

That's not a bug report. That's the beta doing exactly what it's supposed to do. You can't simulate a builder sitting on the train on a Samsung phone trying to study between jobs. You only learn that when real people are in the system pushing on every corner of it finding the true user edge cases testing can't find.

This is our first month with real users in the platform, and feedback like Shaf's is why we're already better than we were on day one. There have been a half dozen major insights that have driven changes. Every improvement we've made so far started the same way. A builder flagged something, we acted on it, and the whole community benefited.

So here's what's happening next week, we're rolling out an updated study guide format upgrade across all tiers. The study guide content will display directly inside each lesson as native text that fits any screen. No more downloading a PDF to read the material. Open the lesson, read it right there, on whatever device you're working from, easy to screenshot it for your AI Assistant.

For builders on desktops and laptops who use the PDF for loading into their AI assistants, the PDF library stays. Every study guide remains available as a downloadable file. This new functionality is an addition to improve the life of our mobile-only Faction members, not a replacement.

There will be brief maintenance windows across each tier throughout next week as we implement these exciting changes. Nothing that should interrupt your progress for long, but I wanted to give you a heads up.

[Shaf](https://the-faction.mn.co/members/40648972), thank you for being direct. That's the culture here. If something isn't working, say it. Every builder in this community benefits when one person speaks up.

If anyone else is running into friction, drop it in my DMs.

- Matt
