# OpenAI committed $1 billion to certify 10 million Americans by 2030. Partners in

OpenAI committed $1 billion to certify 10 million Americans by 2030. Partners include Walmart, John Deere, Lowe's, BCG, and Accenture.

It's impressive. It's also the wrong certification for anyone trying to actually deploy AI in their business.

Every program has the same thing in common: they certify tool knowledge. They answer "do you know what this tool does?" They don't answer the question that actually matters: "can you deploy AI in a real business, with real constraints, in a way that produces measurable results?"

I've talked to operators with three, four, five AI certifications who still can't deploy a single working AI workflow in their business. They know the theory. They know the tools. They don't have the implementation knowledge that connects "I understand this" to "this is running in my business saving us 20 hours a week."

A vendor certification is like getting a driver's license. It proves you can operate the vehicle. It says nothing about whether you can navigate to a specific destination in traffic, weather, and construction.

The cert isn't the capability. The capability is the cert.
