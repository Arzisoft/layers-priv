# Why I Joined Faction

**Why I Joined Faction**

I've been building a custom platform for my video marketing agency that connects to clients' **Meta ad accounts** and **Go High Level CRM** to monitor ad fatigue, lead flow, and client responsiveness all in one place. It's grown into something pretty big: full project management, a **gated video review app**, automated **weekly creative audits** that transcribe and analyze video ads with AI, client onboarding automation, retention and LTV tracking, and even system monitoring for uptime and AI credit usage.

I'm already actively using this tool to serve my clients and stay on top of their accounts. It works. But there's a lot I need to clean up: my reports are stored in postgres when they should be in an **R2 bucket**, the app isn't multi-tenant secure yet, and I still need to build out features like a contractor back office and proper database architecture.

I want to turn this into a product I can sell to the -150 other people in my community who run meta ad agencies for their clients. To get there, I need feedback on the development, help making it more secure, and guidance on building out the databases correctly. That's why I'm here.
