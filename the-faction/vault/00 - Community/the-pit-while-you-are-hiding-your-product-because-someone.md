# While you are hiding your product because someone might steal your idea, 10,000

While you are hiding your product because someone might steal your idea, 10,000 people are building the exact same thing. They are shipping. They have not thought about you once. Your idea is not the moat. Execution is. Stop building tools for your competitors. Build weapons that help one company dominate their space.

**PROMPT:** Direct your AI: "Build a competitive reality assessment: market saturation scan with top 10 competitors, differentiation audit comparing my product on 5 dimensions, and a 30-day speed-to-market sprint plan to get in front of paying users."
