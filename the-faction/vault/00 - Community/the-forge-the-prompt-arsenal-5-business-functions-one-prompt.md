# THE PROMPT ARSENAL — 5 Business Functions. One Prompt Library.

THE PROMPT ARSENAL — 5 Business Functions. One Prompt Library.

Most people write prompts from scratch every time they need AI to do something. That's slow, inconsistent, and it means you're rebuilding the wheel daily.

The Prompt Arsenal is a pre-built library organized around the 5 core functions of any business. Instead of starting from zero, you pull the right prompt for the job and go.

FUNCTION 1: MARKETING

Content creation, campaign planning, audience research, SEO, social media copy, email sequences, ad variations. These prompts turn your marketing from guesswork into a system. You feed in your positioning, your audience, your goals — and the AI generates options you can refine.

FUNCTION 2: SALES

Outreach scripts, follow-up sequences, objection handling, proposal drafts, call prep research. The prompts here are designed to make your sales process faster without making it feel automated. Personal. Specific. Direct.

FUNCTION 3: OFFER DESIGN

Pricing strategies, package structures, value proposition refinement, competitive positioning. These prompts help you think through what you're actually selling and how to frame it so the right people say yes.

FUNCTION 4: EXECUTIVE ASSISTANCE

Meeting prep, email drafting, calendar management, research synthesis, decision frameworks. Think of this as your AI chief of staff. The prompts handle the operational overhead so you can focus on the decisions that actually matter.

FUNCTION 5: OPERATIONS

SOP creation, process documentation, team communication templates, project planning, reporting. These prompts systematize the back-office work that eats hours every week.

HOW TO USE IT:

Don't try to build all 5 at once. Start with the function that's eating the most of your time. Build 3-5 prompts for that function. Test them. Refine them. Then move to the next function.

The goal is simple: never write the same prompt twice. Build the arsenal, use it daily, and keep improving it.
