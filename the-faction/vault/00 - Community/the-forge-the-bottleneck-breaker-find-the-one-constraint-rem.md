# THE BOTTLENECK BREAKER — Find the ONE Constraint. Remove It.

THE BOTTLENECK BREAKER — Find the ONE Constraint. Remove It.

Growth doesn't come from doing more. It comes from identifying and removing the ONE constraint that's holding everything back.

Most people try to fix everything at once. They work on marketing, sales, operations, hiring, content, and product simultaneously. The result? Nothing moves meaningfully. You spread thin, burn out, and wonder why 80-hour weeks aren't producing results.

The Bottleneck Breaker flips this. Instead of doing more, you do less — but you do the right thing.

HOW IT WORKS:

STEP 1: MAP YOUR PIPELINE

Lay out every step from first contact to delivered result. For most businesses, this is: Attract → Capture → Qualify → Nurture → Close → Deliver. Write down the numbers at each stage. Where does the biggest drop happen?

STEP 2: FIND THE CONSTRAINT

The constraint is the step where things stall, break, or leak. Maybe you're getting plenty of leads but nobody books a call. Maybe people book calls but don't show up. Maybe they show up but don't buy. The data tells you exactly where to look.

STEP 3: FIX ONLY THAT

Resist the urge to optimize everything. Fix the one thing that unlocks the most throughput. If your constraint is that leads aren't booking calls, don't redesign your website — fix the booking mechanism. If people aren't showing up, fix the reminder sequence.

STEP 4: REPEAT

Once you fix the constraint, a new one appears. That's how it works. The game is always: find the constraint, remove it, find the next one. The business that does this fastest wins.

The question isn't "what should I work on?" The question is "what's the ONE thing that, if fixed, would make everything else easier or unnecessary?"

Answer that. Fix that. Then ask it again.
