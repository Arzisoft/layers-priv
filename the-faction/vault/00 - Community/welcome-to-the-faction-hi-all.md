# Hi All,

Hi All,

Not entirely sure if this is the correct spot but wanted to take a moment and step out of my comfort zone and introduce myself and share what iv been building.

My names Mike, proud father and I think I count as a builder but I'm not sure, everything I know about AI and software design in conjunction with business process implementation is all self taught.

For the last 18 months iv been building EEWT Labs Ltd and the software platform called FIWK.

The core problem we’re tackling is simple:

As LLMs and agentic systems start doing real business tasks, organisations need to prove what the AI was asked to do, whether the workflow drifted from the user’s original intent, who reviewed it, and whether the final output can be trusted.

FIWK runs alongside existing AI systems rather than replacing them. It tracks intent continuity, flags deviation, creates tamper-evident workflow records, and produces verification/certification reports that can be reviewed later.

The current build includes a Chrome extension, web console, real-time deviation tracking, Merkle-style audit ledger, certification flow, and offline verification capability.

The build currently runs across AWS, Azure, Supabase and cloudflare and is a full BYOK platform.

The next step is API gateway for enterprise and real time agentic workflow tracking coming soon!

We’re in alpha now and preparing for wider testing.

I’m here to learn, get challenged, meet technical people working around AI governance/security, and hopefully find people willing to tear the idea apart before the market does.

If anyone has any questions please ask as this is a big step for me, I don't normally do online communities but this one has been invaluable from the Facebook videos alone and has proven its value time and time again.
