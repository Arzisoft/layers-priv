# Your $77 price tag is locking out half the planet.

Your $77 price tag is locking out half the planet.

Here's what I mean. Today we launched the Faction community — $77 a month. Fair price if you're in New York or London. But if you're a builder in São Paulo, Johannesburg, or Sofia? That same $77 might be a week's discretionary income. You just told some of the most resourceful operators on Earth they're not welcome.

That's a strategy problem, not a pricing problem.

Purchasing power parity means you set region-aware price points so the *value exchange* feels equivalent everywhere. Stripe lets you do this at the infrastructure level — same product, same access, different number on the invoice based on local economic reality.

Why this matters beyond being generous: you're expanding your total addressable market overnight. You're converting in regions where US pricing is a non-starter. And more members means stronger network effects — which makes the community more valuable for *everyone*, including the people paying full price.

Stop pricing for one economy. Price for the world.
