# DELETE IF NOT ALLOWED

*DELETE IF NOT ALLOWED*

**NOBODY PROTECTS YOUR VALUE UNTIL YOU DO.**

Learned this one the expensive way.

Early in my client work, I said yes to everything. Handshake scope. "We'll figure out payment as we go." I thought being easy to work with was the same as being valuable. It's not. Being easy to take advantage of just attracts the people who will.

Here's what changed after I got burned:

**Deposits before code.** If a client won't put money down, they've already told you how much they value the work. Believe them.

**Milestone payments, not final invoices.** You don't hand over 3 months of infrastructure and *then* hope the check clears. Payment unlocks the next phase. Every phase.

**IP stays mine until the final payment lands.** The contract says it in plain English. No payment, no ownership. That single clause has more protective power than any awkward conversation ever will.

**Charging what you're worth is a filter, not a barrier.** The clients who balked at real pricing were the same ones who would've nickel-and-dimed every revision, ghosted invoices, and blamed me for their scope creep. Higher pricing didn't cost me clients — it cost me *bad* clients.

The notepad in this photo isn't decoration. Deposits. Milestones. IP. That's the whole lesson in three words.

If you're building for clients and you don't have these locked in your contract, you don't have a business yet you have a hobby that occasionally pays. Dont get burned like I did and waste a years worth of work.
