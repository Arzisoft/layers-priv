# Hey everyone!

Hey everyone!

My name is Cody and I spent 10 years as an athletic performance coach and leader in the human performance industry. So many coaches struggle with collecting data and interpreting data for athletes. Many rely on their spreadsheet skills or softwares that just don’t solve specific problems for every coach.

I have went on the journey of creating tools for coaches based on my own experiences. I built a tool in Google Sheets by hand that close to 500 coaches bought and loved.

I realized how limited I was in a spreadsheet.

The last 6 months I have been diving into vibe coding and absorbing everything about software engineering. I have deployed several tools that are extremely useful in my niche, but I am now committed to learning how to be a real professional in this space. There is a huge need in the market for this, and I am committed to doing this right.

It is difficult to find someone with my experience as a coach and the skillsets to develop real tools that help coaches in the field. I have lived it. I speak their language. And can create high value.

I am here to sharpen my sword, and go from someone with highly valuable ideas to real bonafide shipped products that can help performance coaches, programs, and training facilities!
