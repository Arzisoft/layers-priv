# Hey, I'm Jimmy. I started vibe coding as a way to learn AI and help guide my kid

Hey, I'm Jimmy. I started vibe coding as a way to learn AI and help guide my kids through what's coming. Along the way I realized I could solve a problem in my own industry, digital evidence. I've spent 30 years in investigations, security, and the military. Zero engineering background. So for the last 7 months I've been building, blindly, in my free time.

Matt's videos saved me from publishing a liability more than once. So I'm here to learn and earn the CADE.

What I built: an API that spits out a cryptographically sealed receipt proving a piece of digital data existed at a specific moment and hasn't changed since. Best way I can explain it, think of the newspaper in a hostage photo. That's proof-of-life for the hostage. This is point-in-time proof for your data. Every receipt gets anchored to independent public information that existed only at that moment, stuff nobody could recreate after the fact. It proves possession, existence, time, and integrity. On purpose, it never claims truth, authorship, or identity. That line matters in my world. Right now it runs as a Chrome extension for capturing web pages, with the API underneath doing the sealing.
