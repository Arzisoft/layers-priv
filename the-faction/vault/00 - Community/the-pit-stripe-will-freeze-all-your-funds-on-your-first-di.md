# Stripe will freeze all your funds on your first dispute. Not some. All. Your AI

Stripe will freeze all your funds on your first dispute. Not some. All. Your AI connected the checkout and never configured dispute protection.

Today I walk through three things you direct your AI to build: a published refund policy, chargeback threshold alerts, and a dispute response workflow with pre-loaded templates.

-MM

**ORCHESTRATION PROMPT**

Direct your AI: "I am running a SaaS subscription product on Stripe. Build me a dispute protection system with three components: (1) A published refund policy page that matches my actual refund terms, linked from the checkout page. (2) Chargeback threshold monitoring that alerts me via email when my dispute rate exceeds 0.5% and again at 0.75%. (3) A dispute response workflow template that includes transaction logs, delivery confirmation screenshots, refund policy evidence, and a response timeline checklist. Output the refund policy as HTML and the dispute response as a reusable template document."
