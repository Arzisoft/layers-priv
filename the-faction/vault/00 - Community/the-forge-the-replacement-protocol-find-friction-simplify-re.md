# THE REPLACEMENT PROTOCOL — Find Friction. Simplify. Replace Yourself.

THE REPLACEMENT PROTOCOL — Find Friction. Simplify. Replace Yourself.

Every task you're still doing manually is a choice. And most of the time, it's the wrong one.

The Replacement Protocol is a 3-step cycle that systematically removes you from low-value work. It's not complicated. It's just disciplined.

STEP 1: FIND

Where are you the bottleneck? Track your time for one week. Be brutally honest. Circle every task that repeats — email responses, scheduling, data entry, report generation, follow-ups. These are the targets.

STEP 2: SIMPLIFY

Can you explain the task in 5 steps or less? If not, break it down further. No AI can automate chaos. You have to simplify before you can systematize. Write the SOP. Make it clear enough that someone — or something — else could follow it.

STEP 3: REPLACE

Build the automation or deploy the agent. Hand it off. You just bought back your time. The goal isn't to optimize how you do the task. The goal is to never touch it again.

Then REPEAT. Go back to Step 1 and find the next bottleneck. There's always another one.

What to look for first: email, scheduling, reporting. These three are almost always automatable today.

The test is simple: could you hand this SOP to someone — or something — and have them execute it without calling you? If yes, you shouldn't be doing it. Full stop.
