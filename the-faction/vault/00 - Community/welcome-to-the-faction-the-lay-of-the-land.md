# THE LAY OF THE LAND

THE LAY OF THE LAND

You just joined a community of people building real things with AI.

This isn't a course platform in the usual sense. This isn't a Facebook group. This is 77 courses covering the AI-Directed Engineering stack, plus a room full of builders pushing each other to get better along the way.

Here's how everything is structured.

WHAT'S HERE

Seventy-seven courses across six tiers. All free. All open right now.

Every course follows the same shape: a study guide you read on the page, then an exam that checks whether it actually landed. Pass the exam, earn the badge. That's the whole system.

Nothing is locked behind finishing something else. Start wherever makes sense for what you're building.

Tiers 2 and 3 are temporarily offline while we restructure them.

THE CURRICULUM

The Foundation — Tier 1. The thirteen layers of the AI-Directed Engineering stack. Most people start here.

The Mastery — Tier 4. Build a production SaaS end to end.

The Industry — Tier 5. How education, construction and healthcare actually run, and the compliance lines you can't cross.

The Vault — Tier 6. Security, top to bottom.

The Launchpad — Tier 7. Finding clients, pricing your builds, and the legal and financial basics of turning this into a business.

The Frontier — Tier 8. Agent orchestration and advanced systems.

THE COMMUNITY

The Pit — The main floor. Daily conversation, wins, challenges, and real talk. This is where the crew lives.

The Forge — Framework vault. Reference material, manuscripts, and the tools behind every layer of the stack.

The Mentoring Lounge — Bring your problems. This is where you get unstuck.

YOUR FIRST MOVES

1. Drop a comment on the pinned post above this one — introduce yourself and tell us what you're building.

2. Head to The Pit and connect with the crew.

3. Open The Foundation and start Layer 01.

That's it. No 47-step onboarding sequence. No welcome webinar. You're in a room full of people who build things.

Welcome to The Faction. Now get out there and build something.
