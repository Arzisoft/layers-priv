# HEY FACTION!

HEY FACTION!

Welcome to the Mentoring Lounge.

This is the room I wish existed when I was building my first product, somewhere between "I watched the tutorial on Youtube" and "holy shit, it's live and people are now paying for it."

That gap? It's where most builders stall out. Not because they can't code or can't ship. Because they're making decisions in a vacuum. Pricing. Architecture trade-offs. Whether to rebuild or patch. When to launch ugly. The stuff that doesn't show up in a module or a YouTube video.

That's what this space is for.

The Mentoring Lounge is where experienced builders sit down with newer ones. No scripts. No office hours with a countdown timer. Just real conversations between people who've shipped and people who are about to.

If you've been building for a while or you've launched something, broken something in production, figured out what "good enough" actually means, your perspective matters here, a lot. Not as a guru. As someone who's been in the seat, who has done the reps, earned the scars.

If you're earlier in the journey or maybe you just finished a module, maybe you're mid-build and second-guessing everything, this is where you ask the questions you can't Google. The ones that start with "would you…" or "how did you decide…"

A few things this space is NOT:

→ It's not a help desk. We've got other spaces for troubleshooting.

→ It's not a lecture hall. Nobody's here to teach at you.

→ It's not a networking event with name tags. We're builders, not LinkedIn.

It IS the place where someone three steps ahead of you pulls up a chair and says, "yeah, I hit that exact wall and here's what I did."

So let's start there.

Drop a comment below or start a fresh new thread and introduce yourself:

— What are you building right now?

— Where are you stuck in the process (idea stage, mid-build, launched, scaling)?

— What's the one question you keep circling back to or need help with?

I'll go first: I'm Matt. I run The Faction. I've built, broken, and rebuilt more products than I can count. The question I keep coming back to, even now with this community, is "am I building what people actually want, or what I think they should want?"

Turns out that question never fully goes away. But it gets easier to answer when you're not asking it alone.

Welcome in. Grab a seat.

Matt Murphy💯👊😎
