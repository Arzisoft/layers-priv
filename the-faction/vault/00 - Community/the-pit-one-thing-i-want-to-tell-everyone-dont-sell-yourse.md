# One thing I want to tell everyone: don't sell yourself short.

One thing I want to tell everyone: don't sell yourself short.

The other night, I decided to stop overthinking and just take the tests. No endless studying. No waiting until I felt "ready." I read the questions, reviewed the answers, trusted what I've learned, and chose the best fit.

Before every test, I was convinced I might fail.

Then I passed one.

Then another.

Then another.

With every passing score, my confidence grew. I moved faster. I trusted myself more.

What I realized is that the confidence wasn't built by thinking about it. It was built by doing it.

For the last three years, I've been relentlessly focused on learning, building, failing, improving, and growing. Day after day, even when it didn't feel like progress was happening.

The lesson?

You're probably more capable than you think you are.

The knowledge you've been collecting matters. The work you've been putting in matters. The late nights, the failures, the repetition—it all compounds.

Sometimes the only thing standing between you and the next level is believing that you're already closer than you think.

Bet on yourself. Trust the work. Take the test.
