# THE PIPELINE PROTOCOL — 8 Steps From Stranger to Signed

THE PIPELINE PROTOCOL — 8 Steps From Stranger to Signed

Most people think sales is about pitching. It's not. Sales is about building a system where people sell themselves before they ever get on a call with you. The Pipeline Protocol is that system — 8 steps, 3 phases, zero cold calls.

PHASE 1: INBOUND

Step 1 — ATTRACT: Your content does the heavy lifting. Every post, every reel, every value drop is pulling the right people toward you. You're not chasing. You're attracting.

Step 2 — CAPTURE: A keyword in the DMs triggers the conversation. They raise their hand. You didn't cold message anyone.

PHASE 2: CONVERT

Step 3 — QUALIFY: Before you invest a single minute, 4 questions filter whether this person is actually a fit. Time is the asset — protect it.

Step 4 — NURTURE: Automated value delivery keeps building trust while you sleep. They're getting smarter about what you do without you lifting a finger.

Step 5 — BOOK: Calendly link. They pick a time. No back-and-forth scheduling emails.

PHASE 3: DELIVER

Step 6 — PREP: AI researches them before you show up. You walk into the call knowing their business, their challenges, their opportunity.

Step 7 — CLOSE: The call is a formality. They've been nurtured, qualified, and prepped. You're just confirming the fit.

Step 8 — ONBOARD: Automated kickoff. Zero manual follow-up. The experience starts before they second-guess the decision.

The whole point is this: by the time someone gets to Step 7, they've already made the decision. You just need to not mess it up. Build the pipeline once, run it forever.
