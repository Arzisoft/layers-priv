# I’m finally dropping in to say hello! I've been on the periphery since late June

I’m finally dropping in to say hello! I've been on the periphery since late June, which I would defend as an ‘observation’ period, but more accurately described as ‘stalling’. In fairness, during that time, I have been consuming Matt’s videos the way fat kid eats hamburgers! So, to avoid what very well could be retraining order, I figured it would be best to not be so antisocial!!!

My Philosophy:

I've always believed in training my replacement. You don't own a process by being the only person who can run it, and I've never been interested in job security that depends on nobody else understanding my spreadsheet. There is no growth in gatekeeping!

In the last couple of years, what's changed is who the replacement is. More and more, my successor is automated — and I think that's fantastic!!! It also means the honest response is to learn, adapt then move toward it…not to defend a seat…BUT…to find my new one!

Building before I found Matt's work was hard!! Specifically, I was reverse-engineering, mostly in the middle of the night, from a position of not knowing what I didn't know. I wish I'd found this, Matt, a year ago. I am here for it now! I reasonably confident in what I've built so far…but super excited to see what's coming!!

I hope that didn't sound like a dating profile - I am particularly long winded today!
