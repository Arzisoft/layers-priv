Your AI will build you a login page in four minutes. It will never tell you to register the business behind it.

Today I walk through the three things you direct your AI to set up before you take your first dollar. Entity structure. Operating agreement. Pre-revenue checklist.

None of them are technical. All of them protect you.

Your AI built a solid product. No doubt about it. But it did not register your business. Your house, your car, your savings, all of it at risk. Every dollar your app collects before you set up your LLC, Tricky.

Because you do not have an LLC, you also don't have an EIN, you do not have a business bank account, and you do not have a registered business entity in any given state. That's a no no. So here are the three things you direct your AI to build before you take a single dollar. Number one, direct your AI to research entity structures for your specific state. LLC versus S Corp versus sole proprietorship.

Most builders default to LLC because someone on Twitter said to, but your tax situation, your state filing requirements, and whether you plan to take on investors all change that answer. Your AI can lay out the comparison, you make the call. That's a win. Step two, direct your AI to draft your operating agreement. Most builders do not know this document exists, but you need to know.

It defines how your business runs, how your profits move, and what happens when things go sideways. Some states require it just to open a business bank account. Your bank will ask for it before they give you a merchant account. So it's on the list. You got to have it.

Without it, you're not even a business. You're just a person who built an app. And step three, direct your AI to build a pre revenue checklist. EIN application, state filing, registered agent, business bank account, and insurance. Every one of these has to be done before you take payment from a single customer and not one of them is technical.

These are all operational. Your AI will never bring them up because they are not engineering problems. They are business problems that you're responsible for. Your AI will build you a login page in four minutes. It will never tell you to register the business that's behind it.

That decision is all yours. Make it before your first customer makes it for you.
