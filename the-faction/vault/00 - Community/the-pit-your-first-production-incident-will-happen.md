# Your first production incident will happen.

Your first production incident will happen.

That is not the problem.

Having no playbook for what happens after is the problem.

Here are three things you direct your AI to build right now.

Step one.

The post-mortem template.

Direct your AI to create a five-field template before your first incident.

What happened. What was the impact. Root cause. What fixed it. What prevents it next time.

This exists before anything breaks. Not during the panic.

Step two.

The 48-hour rule.

Every incident gets a post-mortem within forty-eight hours.

Not as blame. As systems improvement.

The question is never who broke it. It is what process allowed this to reach production.

Direct your AI to schedule the review automatically when an incident is logged.

Skip the review and the same failure repeats in six months.

Step three.

The incident library.

Every post-mortem adds to a knowledge base.

Direct your AI to store them and reference them when similar patterns appear.

The same root cause never produces the same outage twice.

The companies that run post-mortems get better.

The ones that skip them repeat the same failures on a cycle.

Your AI can build the template, schedule the review, and maintain the library.

You just have to tell it to.

-MM

**ORCHESTRATION PROMPT: **

*Create a post-mortem template with five fields including what happened, impact assessment, root cause analysis, remediation steps, and prevention measures. Build an incident logging system that automatically schedules a post-mortem review within 48 hours. Create an incident knowledge base that stores all completed post-mortems searchable by root cause category and failure pattern.*
