# THE INTAKE ENGINE — 3 Steps to Never Miss a Message Again

THE INTAKE ENGINE — 3 Steps to Never Miss a Message Again

Every business runs on communication. And most businesses are drowning in it. Emails, DMs, Slack messages, texts, voicemails — coming from every direction, all day, with no system to process any of it.

The result: things fall through cracks. Leads go cold. Team members wait on answers. Clients feel ignored. And you spend your day reacting instead of leading.

The Intake Engine fixes this with a 3-step protocol:

STEP 1: INPUT

Every message — regardless of channel — gets captured into ONE intake system. Not your inbox. Not your DMs. Not your brain. A single intake point where everything lands before it gets processed.

This is the critical shift: stop processing messages where they arrive. Route them to where they get handled.

STEP 2: CONNECT

Each message gets tagged and routed to the right person or system. Client question? Goes to the client team. Sales inquiry? Goes to your pipeline. Internal request? Gets a task created. Bug report? Goes to the dev queue.

The key: YOU are not the router. The system is. If every message has to pass through you before it reaches the right person, you are the bottleneck. Build the routing rules once. Let the system handle the traffic.

STEP 3: REVIEW

At the end of each day (or twice a day if volume is high), you review what came in, what was routed, and what needs your attention. This is a 15-minute scan, not a 3-hour email session.

You're looking for: anything escalated to you, anything that fell through the routing rules, and any patterns that suggest you need a new route.

The difference between business owners who are always overwhelmed and those who seem calm? The calm ones have an intake engine. They're not smarter or less busy. They just have a system that handles the noise so they can focus on the signal.

Build the intake. Route the messages. Review the exceptions. That's it.
