# Here is something I would never put on Instagram because it would start a war.

Here is something I would never put on Instagram because it would start a war.

Free tier is a business strategy. Not a gift.
Every platform that gives you a free tier is making a bet that you will grow into a paying customer before you grow out of their platform.

And most of the time they are right.

Here is what I tell clients.
The free tier is not free. It is pre-paid. You pay with switching costs later.

When you build your auth on their platform, your database on their platform, your storage on their platform you are not saving money. You are buying convenience today with flexibility tomorrow.

That is not a bad deal. But it is a deal. And you need to know the terms before you sign.

The question I ask every builder in a scoping session is this: at what user count does this architecture break?

Not crash. Not slow down. Break.

If you cannot answer that question, you are not building on a free tier.

You are building on a timer and it’s going to run out.

Know the number.
