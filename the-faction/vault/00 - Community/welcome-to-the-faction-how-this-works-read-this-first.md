# HOW THIS WORKS — Read This First

HOW THIS WORKS — Read This First

Welcome. Whether you're here for the frameworks, the courses, or just to be around people who are actually building — you're in the right place. Let me walk you through how this whole thing is structured so you can hit the ground running.

This is not a video library. There are no lectures to sit through.

The Faction is 77 courses covering the AI-Directed Engineering stack, and every one of them is open to you right now. The format is simple: study guides you read right here on the page, then an exam that tests whether you actually absorbed the material.

Here's the flow for every layer:

Go into The Foundation. Pick a layer — say Layer 01, Frontend Foundations. Inside that layer you'll see a study guide. It opens right on the page; there is nothing to download. Read it. Take your time with it. When you feel like you've got a handle on the material, come back and hit the exam. The exam questions you on everything in that study guide — not trivia, real comprehension of the concepts and what you'd actually do with them. Pass it and you earn the badge.

Everything is open. Go at your own pace.

Seventy-seven courses across six tiers, all free, all unlocked. Nothing is gated behind finishing something else. Most people start at The Foundation because the upper tiers build on what it establishes — but if you want to jump straight into The Vault or The Frontier on day one, go.

Tiers 2 and 3 are temporarily offline while we restructure them.

IMPORTANT — Join every space you see.

When you first arrive, not all spaces may show up in your left sidebar. Take a moment to scroll the space list and click Join on each one you don't see. That makes sure everything is in your sidebar and easy to get to.

The curriculum:

The Foundation — Tier 1. All 13 layers at foundational depth. Start here.

The Mastery — Tier 4. Build a production SaaS end to end.

The Industry — Tier 5. Education, construction and healthcare verticals.

The Vault — Tier 6. Security, top to bottom.

The Launchpad — Tier 7. Getting paid for what you build.

The Frontier — Tier 8. Agent orchestration and advanced systems.

The community:

The Pit — Talk shop, share work, ask questions.

The Forge — Framework vault. Reference material and manuscripts.

The Mentoring Lounge — Bring your problems.

Badges are how you track what you've actually absorbed. Pass an exam, earn the badge. That's the whole system — no gates, no prerequisites, no waiting.

If you're staring at a layer and don't see a video, that's by design. Read the study guide. Then prove you know it.

Let's get to work.

  > Frank, this is exactly the right response, and honestly, the fact that you can name the gap this clearly tells me you’re already thinking like the operator of the system, not just the person building it.
  > 
  > Authorization and auditability give you control and evidence. **Failure containment gives you survivability.** That is the difference between one agent making a bad call and one agent contaminating the entire company OS.
  > 
  > I like the direction you’re taking: assume every agent, function, and integration can eventually fail, then design the boundaries so the blast radius stays small. Separate tenant data, scoped permissions, isolated queues, idempotent actions, timeouts, rollback paths, circuit breakers, and human escalation for anything irreversible. Don’t rely on every function being written perfectly, make the architecture expect imperfection.

Retrofitting containment after ten customer types and eighteen agents are moving real data is how teams accidentally create a monster. 😂
  > 
  > Keep stress-testing it and post what breaks. This is proper AI-directed engineering: not asking whether the agents work when everything goes right, but designing what happens when one confidently drives into the ditch. 👊😎
